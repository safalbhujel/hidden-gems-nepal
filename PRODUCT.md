# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Travelers (both local and international) seeking authentic, uncrowded spots in Nepal, and locals sharing their discoveries.

## Product Purpose
HiddenGems Nepal connects people with the real Nepal—hidden waterfalls, cozy local cafés, quiet sunset viewpoints, and authentic food spots—helping them discover places worth visiting while reducing overtourism in popular spots.

## Positioning
An intent-based discovery platform rather than a generic directory. It uses a signature "Hidden Score" to estimate how under-the-radar a place is, allowing users to find spots based on their "vibe" and location.

## Operating Context
Users search by mood, category, and vibe using intuitive tags. They explore visually on an interactive Mapbox map with "near me" functionality powered by PostGIS radius queries.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Tech Stack: Next.js 14+, Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Storage, PostGIS), Tailwind CSS v4, Mapbox GL JS.
- Rich media support (up to 6 images per place, optional video embeds).
- Admin moderation queue required for community submissions.

## Brand Commitments
"Discover the Nepal that everyone misses."

## Evidence on Hand
Live demo at https://hiddengems-nepal.vercel.app. Open source codebase.

## Product Principles
1. **Authenticity Over Popularity:** Surface places that are genuinely unique and under-the-radar.
2. **Intent-Driven Exploration:** Let users search by the experience they want, not just the name of a place.
3. **Community-Powered Quality:** Rely on real photos and tips from locals, moderated for accuracy.
